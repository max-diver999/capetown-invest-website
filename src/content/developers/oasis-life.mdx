## What Oasis Life actually is

A hospitality operator that happens to house people permanently.

Rabie launched the brand in 2018 and it now runs four estates across the Western Cape. The detail that explains the rest is the leadership: Glyn Taylor became chief executive in 2023 after serving as joint chief executive of the Century City Conference Centre and Hotel, and the company says directly that its standards rest on hotel-sector experience.

That is not a decorative fact. A property developer builds, sells and leaves. A hotel operator stays, because the revenue is in the running rather than in the sale. The life rights model follows from that logic rather than from a financing preference: the operator keeps the buildings because operating them is the business.

Clara Anna Fontein is the one worth understanding first, because it is not a standalone village. The Oasis Life units sit inside a Rabie estate that also holds 126 townhouses and 344 plot-and-plan erven, with residents sharing the estate gym, pool, tennis and squash courts while keeping their own clubhouse. Multi-generational is usually a slogan; there it describes a site plan.

Insider tip: on Sunningdale, note that the sales are handled by an outside agency rather than by Oasis Life directly. That is normal for a launch phase and it changes who you are actually negotiating with, so establish early which party can answer contract questions.

## Why the Lifestyle Centre arrives first

Because a retirement village without one is a housing estate.

Every Oasis Life estate gets its Lifestyle Centre or Clubhouse in the first phase. The centre carries a clubhouse with a restaurant, a deli and a bar, spaces for fitness, health and leisure, and primary healthcare consulting rooms in the same building.

On many retirement developments the communal building arrives with the final phase, which means the earliest residents buy a community that does not yet exist and live on a construction site while waiting for it. Delivering it first is expensive and it is the clearest evidence that the operator is thinking about occupancy rather than about sales velocity.

Three things to verify on any estate you visit, here or elsewhere:

1. **Which phase the centre sits in**, in writing.
2. **What the primary healthcare covers**, and what happens when care needs rise past it.
3. **Who staffs the hospitality**, and on what contract, because that is what you are paying the levy for.

## The Four Security Pillars, and which one to test

Lifestyle, health and wellbeing, personal safety, finances. Only one is contractual.

Oasis Life organises its promise around four pillars. Lifestyle security is the resort-style hospitality and freedom from maintenance. Financial security, in the company's own framing, is the security of tenure a life right provides for the rest of the holder's life.

The first three pillars are delivered by operations and are visible on a visit: you can eat in the restaurant, look at the consulting rooms, and see the security arrangement. The fourth is a document, and it is the one that decides what happens to your capital. That asymmetry is the practical reason to spend your due diligence on the agreement rather than on the amenities, however good the amenities are.

## What this means for a buyer, and for a family

The advantages and the disadvantages both come from the same design.

The advantages: homes that are pet-friendly, futureproofed and age-tailored; hospitality delivered by people who have run hotels; a centre that exists from day one; primary healthcare on the same site; and freedom from every maintenance obligation, including the ones that become genuinely difficult in a person's eighties. The disadvantages: no title, no bond, no free letting, and an exit set by a contract rather than by the market. A family expecting to inherit an appreciating asset is expecting the wrong thing, and that conversation is better held before the loan is advanced than after.

For anyone arriving from abroad, the housing decision and the immigration decision are separate and do not help each other. The [residency guide](/guides/does-buying-property-give-residency-south-africa/) and the [retirement visa guide](/guides/retirement-visa-south-africa-property/) cover the visa position, which is unaffected by whether you buy a life right or a house.
