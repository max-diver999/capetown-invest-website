## Estate layout and levy heterogeneity

Royal Ascot spreads roughly 1,749 units across about 20 separate sectional title schemes on one 83-hectare site, which means 20 body corporates and 20 levy trajectories inside a single address. An estate-wide yield average is close to meaningless: two identical apartments on opposite sides can carry levies differing by 30 percent, which is the gap between a working net yield and a disappointing one.

- 20 body corporates, 20 sets of financials, 20 reserve funds
- Levy spread of up to 30 percent between schemes on the same estate
- Special resolutions need 75% under the STSMA, so read the last two sets of minutes

What drives the divergence is scheme-specific: whether the scheme installed generators or inverters and how it funded them, whether it has a pool and landscaped common areas to maintain, the age of the buildings within the phased rollout, the unit count sharing the fixed costs, and any special levy raised for security upgrades or external repairs. Smaller schemes spread fixed costs over fewer owners and tend to run higher per unit.

Buy at the scheme level, not the estate level. Identify the exact scheme name from the title deed first, then request three years of audited body corporate financials, the levy roll, the ten-year maintenance plan against the current reserve balance, and the minutes of the last two general meetings. Under the STSMA a special resolution requires 75%, so read those minutes for anything pending that could commit owners to capital spend. The fynbos set-aside and the mature streetscapes are estate-wide goods. The levy that pays for your building is not.

Anyone asking for a reservation deposit on Infinity Milnerton is selling something that does not exist. Underwrite the real building as a seasonal asset with 2 months of winter void.

The distinction matters beyond the naming, because the two locations are different investment products. Coral Road is beachfront: the tenant pool skews toward lifestyle renters and seasonal short-stay, the views carry a premium, and coastal exposure brings salt corrosion, higher maintenance on external elements, and insurance that reflects wind and water risk. Estate stock in Milnerton is inland-facing and family-oriented, with a long-let tenant who commutes to Century City or the CBD.

If beachfront is genuinely what you want, underwrite it as a seasonal asset. Add winter void months to your model, confirm the body corporate's position on short-stay letting before you assume nightly income, and read the maintenance history specifically for facade, balcony, and window replacement cycles, which run harder this close to the water. Then compare it against a Milnerton estate unit on net cash flow, not on the photograph. Coastal context sits in the [Blouberg area guide](/areas/blouberg-property-investment/).

## Transfer duty and ticket sizes on resale

Ticket size drives the tax bill here more than any other variable, and this is where offshore buyers most often underestimate the cash needed on transfer day. Read the numbers below alongside the levy analysis above, because duty is a one-off and levies are permanent, and the two together decide whether a Royal Ascot resale at R1.2 million or at R4 million clears your target net yield.

Transfer duty on Royal Ascot resale is the line most buyers get wrong, because the estate spans two very different tax positions. A compact apartment near R1.2 million attracts duty only on the slice above R1,210,000, so the cash cost is close to nothing, while a R4 million townhouse sits well up the sliding scale and carries a charge running into six figures that no bond will cover. Duty falls due in cash before lodgement, alongside conveyancing fees, which makes the gap between a R1.3 million and a R2.8 million ticket wider than the price difference alone suggests. Resale stock carries transfer duty rather than VAT: the 15% VAT treatment applies only where a VAT-registered developer is the seller, which on a largely completed estate is now rare. Confirm the seller's VAT status in writing before you rely on either treatment.
