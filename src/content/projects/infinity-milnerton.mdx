## Estate layout and levy heterogeneity

The single most useful fact about this estate for an investor is structural: roughly 1,749 units sit across about 20 separate sectional title schemes on the 83-hectare site. That is 20 body corporates, 20 sets of financials, 20 reserve funds, and 20 different levy trajectories inside one address. An estate-wide yield average is therefore close to meaningless. Two apartments of identical size on opposite sides of the same estate can carry levies that differ by a third, and that gap is the difference between a working net yield and a disappointing one.

What drives the divergence is scheme-specific: whether the scheme installed generators or inverters and how it funded them, whether it has a pool and landscaped common areas to maintain, the age of the buildings within the phased rollout, the unit count sharing the fixed costs, and any special levy raised for security upgrades or external repairs. Smaller schemes spread fixed costs over fewer owners and tend to run higher per unit.

Buy at the scheme level, not the estate level. Identify the exact scheme name from the title deed first, then request three years of audited body corporate financials, the levy roll, the ten-year maintenance plan against the current reserve balance, and the minutes of the last two general meetings. Under the STSMA a special resolution requires 75%, so read those minutes for anything pending that could commit owners to capital spend. The fynbos set-aside and the mature streetscapes are estate-wide goods. The levy that pays for your building is not.

## Infinity apartments on Coral Road (Bloubergstrand)

The Infinity name on the portals does not lead to a Rabie launch in Milnerton. It usually resolves to Infinity Apartments, a completed beachfront complex at 89 Coral Road in Bloubergstrand, which sits west of Milnerton along the Table Bay coast. It is resale stock in an existing body corporate, not off-plan, so anyone asking you for a reservation deposit on Infinity Milnerton is either confused or selling you something that does not exist.

The distinction matters beyond the naming, because the two locations are different investment products. Coral Road is beachfront: the tenant pool skews toward lifestyle renters and seasonal short-stay, the views carry a premium, and coastal exposure brings salt corrosion, higher maintenance on external elements, and insurance that reflects wind and water risk. Estate stock in Milnerton is inland-facing and family-oriented, with a long-let tenant who commutes to Century City or the CBD.

If beachfront is genuinely what you want, underwrite it as a seasonal asset. Add winter void months to your model, confirm the body corporate's position on short-stay letting before you assume nightly income, and read the maintenance history specifically for facade, balcony, and window replacement cycles, which run harder this close to the water. Then compare it against a Milnerton estate unit on net cash flow, not on the photograph. Coastal context sits in the [Blouberg area guide](/areas/blouberg-property-investment/).

## Who should buy Royal Ascot Milnerton stock and who should look elsewhere?

Royal Ascot suits family owner-occupiers and long-let semigrators who want Rabie-managed precinct maturity, golf and fynbos amenity, and Century City within 5 to 10 minutes, with MODELED Milnerton apartments near 7 percent gross and 5 percent net before scheme-specific levies. Income-first buyers should compare [SkyWater](/projects/skywater-century-city/) and [On Park](/projects/on-park-century-city/) before paying estate premiums, because twenty sectional title schemes each carry different levies and a 7 percent estate average can become weak net if your scheme levies sit 30 percent above a neighbour. Foreign buyers face no surcharge and may finance roughly 50 percent locally, but all stock is resale with transfer duty on the SARS sliding scale. Avoid off-plan deposits on unverified Infinity Milnerton listings: no Rabie Infinity launch exists in Milnerton, and Infinity Apartments at 89 Coral Road is Bloubergstrand beach stock, not this corridor.

**Pros:** Rabie-managed precinct maturity and SAPOA recognition; Century City and Canal Walk within minutes; broad stock from apartments to townhouses; MODELED yields above Atlantic Seaboard on many units; no foreign buyer surcharge.

**Cons:** Twenty schemes mean levy diligence is mandatory; not a single new off-plan launch with uniform pricing; estate homes can show lower net yield than City Bowl apartments; transfer duty on all resale stock; Infinity Milnerton marketing online is often misleading.

**The income investor** targets Milnerton apartments near Century City; compare SkyWater before paying estate premiums. **The family owner-occupier** wants golf, fynbos, and security with yield secondary. **The foreign buyer** records offshore funds cleanly and models transfer duty via the [cost guide](/guides/cost-of-buying-property-cape-town/).

Royal Ascot's golf and fynbos positioning attracts semigration families who want Table Bay lifestyle without Atlantic Seaboard tickets, supporting long-let demand for three-bedroom townhouses even when one-bedroom yields look softer on paper. SAPOA's innovative excellence shortlist signals institutional design discipline on an 83-hectare greenfield that could have fragmented into spec builds. Rabie's development-manager role meant consistent streetscapes and phased services, the same logic behind repeat sell-outs in [Century City](/guides/century-city-property-investment-guide/). When you compare offers, ask for three years of levy increases in the specific scheme, not the estate brochure, because special levies for security or generator work can shift net yield by a full percentage point without changing headline rent.

## How should Milnerton buyers model Royal Ascot versus Century City supply?

Run three cases on the exact scheme you are targeting: base case with trailing 12-month rent, current levy, 5 percent void, and 6 percent annual levy inflation; Century City supply case shaving 5 percent off rent if two new Rabie towers hand over within 24 months; and Blouberg summer case adding two months partial void and higher coastal rates if you compare Infinity beach stock on Coral Road. Document results beside your offer so agent narratives about Infinity Milnerton do not overwrite your underwriting file. Royal Ascot resale spans roughly R1.2 million compact apartments through R4 million plus townhouses, with transfer duty climbing on the SARS sliding scale above R2 million.
