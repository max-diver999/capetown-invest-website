## The suburb the marketing skips

Richwood, not Cape Town, and the difference is the whole comparable set.

Balwin's development index lists the location as Cape Town. The development page names Richwood. Both are technically true, and only one of them lets a buyer do research, because Richwood is a small northern suburbs pocket with its own price level rather than a stand-in for the metro.

Two practical consequences:

1. **Your comparables are Richwood comparables**, not Cape Town averages, and a metro median will overstate what this stock resells for.
2. **Your tenant pool is local**, drawn from the Milnerton, Bothasig and Burgundy Estate area rather than from the city.

The [northern suburbs guide](/guides/northern-suburbs-cape-town-property/) covers the corridor, the [Milnerton page](/areas/milnerton-property-investment/) covers the nearest area with its own page, and the [suburb price table](/guides/cape-town-property-prices-by-suburb-2026/) shows how far apart the metro's corridors actually sit.

Insider tip: when a developer's index and its own project page give different locations, use the project page. The index is organised for search, and search is organised around the bigger name.

## What Balwin is selling here

Specification and outlook, in a package that repeats across the developer's estates.

Luxury apartments that are Wi-Fi ready with eco-friendly appliances, Table Mountain views, and a natural Cape indigenous garden, positioned as connectivity plus comfort. The three-bedroom units carry a virtual tour, which is genuinely useful on a phased release where the show unit and your unit may be in different buildings.

Table Mountain views from the northern suburbs are a real amenity and a fragile one. Unlike a park frontage, a distant mountain outlook can be built out by whatever rises between you and it, so check the approved development around your building rather than the view from the show unit.

Short-term letting is prohibited, as across Balwin's portfolio, with the developer's own wording on our [Suikerbos page](/projects/suikerbos-balwin/) and the mechanism in the [body corporate Airbnb rules guide](/guides/body-corporate-airbnb-ban-rules/). The income model is long-term rent, which the [long-term rental guide](/guides/long-term-rental-cape-town-guide/) covers.

## Advantages and disadvantages

Availability is the advantage; the naming is the disadvantage.

The advantages: Classic Collection stock still releasing in a part of the northern suburbs where [De Aan-Zicht](/projects/de-aan-zicht-balwin/) has already sold out its Classic phases, a 2026 entry at R1,479,900 with no attorney or transfer costs, and a specification consistent across the developer's estates. The disadvantages: a suburb small enough that comparables are thin, a mountain view that is not protected by anything, no short-let option, and the usual Balwin pattern of many similar units arriving in one market together.

## What decides the net

Four lines, one of them provisional.

An agent's fee sits at 8% to 10% of collected rent, one void month across 12 months is prudent, municipal rates are billed against the valuation roll rather than the purchase price, and the body corporate levy on a new scheme is a projection until the scheme has traded. The [levies guide](/guides/sectional-title-levies-cape-town/) sets out what to ask for.

For a foreign buyer, a first sale by the developer wraps 15% VAT into the figure quoted and no duty arises, while a non-resident is usually funded to about 50% of value, putting roughly R740,000 in cash behind a one-bedroom before costs. The full Western Cape range is on the [Balwin Properties page](/developers/balwin-properties/), with [De Kuile](/projects/de-kuile-kuils-river/) as the nearest comparable at a slightly lower entry.
