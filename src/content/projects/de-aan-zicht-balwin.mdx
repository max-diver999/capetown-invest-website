Quick answer: De Aan-Zicht is a Balwin development where the Classic Collection is sold out and the Signature Collection at the Lifestyle Centre runs from R2,749,900 with the last apartment now selling. The fact that should decide your interest is not the view. It is that short-term letting is banned at the development, so the nightly-rate model is off the table and the yield has to work on long-term rent alone.

## What is actually still available

Almost nothing from the developer, which is the first thing to price.

| Tier | What it is | Price | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Classic Collection | The bulk of the estate, one, two and three-bed apartments | Not listed | Sold out |
| Signature Collection, Lifestyle Centre | 22 exclusive apartments with panoramic views | From R2,749,900 | Last apartment now selling |

A buyer arriving today is therefore choosing between the final Signature unit and the resale market, and those are different transactions with different cost stacks. Balwin advertises no attorney fees and no transfer costs on a developer sale. A private resale carries transfer duty on the SARS scale plus conveyancing, which the [cost calculator](/guides/transfer-duty-calculator/) works out for any price.

Put a number on the difference before deciding which route to take. A developer sale from a VAT-registered developer carries 15% VAT inside the price and no transfer duty at all. A resale at the Signature tier's R2,749,900 falls in the 8% transfer duty band, and Balwin's cover of attorney and transfer costs does not travel with a private seller. For a non-resident there is a second constraint on top: South African banks typically cap lending near 50% loan-to-value, so the cash line moves rather than the cost line.

That gap is the same structural point that applies across sold-out schemes: the route that closed was cheaper at the front end than the route that remains. The [off-plan guide](/guides/off-plan-property-cape-town-guide/) covers cessions before transfer and resales after occupation, which are the two ways in once a release is gone.

## The rule that decides the investment case

No short-term letting. The developer says so directly, and it is not a footnote.

The prohibition is Balwin's standard one, applied here as it is across the developer's schemes, and the [Suikerbos page](/projects/suikerbos-balwin/) carries the developer's own wording of what counts as a short stay. What matters at De Aan-Zicht is that it applies from the outset rather than arriving later by resolution.

Three consequences follow, and they are all arithmetic rather than opinion:

1. **The nightly-rate model is gone.** Whatever a comparable Sea Point or City Bowl flat earns on short lets is irrelevant here, so do not import that yield.
2. **Long-term rent is the only income line**, which means the yield is set by monthly market rent for the unit type in the surrounding area, and the [long-term rental guide](/guides/long-term-rental-cape-town-guide/) covers what that looks like.
3. **The rule is a feature for owner-occupiers**, because a building with no nightly turnover is quieter, has fewer strangers in the lifts and tends to have lower common-area wear.

This is the same mechanism the [body corporate Airbnb rules guide](/guides/body-corporate-airbnb-ban-rules/) describes, arriving from the developer rather than from a later special resolution. Getting it up front is better than discovering it at the first AGM, which is the usual way buyers meet this rule. The [short-term rental rules guide](/guides/short-term-rental-rules-cape-town/) covers the City by-law position that sits underneath it.

Insider tip: when a scheme bans short lets from the outset, check whether the rule sits in the conduct rules registered against the scheme or only in the sale agreement. The registered version binds every future owner; a contractual one binds you.

## What the Signature tier is buying you

Twenty-two apartments, and the view is doing most of the pricing.

The Signature Collection at the Lifestyle Centre carries uninterrupted panoramic views over Table Mountain, Lion's Head, Robben Island and the Atlantic. That is a genuinely unusual outlook for a development at this price outside the [Atlantic Seaboard](/guides/atlantic-seaboard-property-investment-guide/), where a comparable panorama costs several times more per square metre. The [suburb price table](/guides/cape-town-property-prices-by-suburb-2026/) sets out what the coastal corridors charge.

The exclusivity is real in the narrow sense that 22 units is a small run inside a large estate, which limits how many identical competing apartments can appear on the resale market at once. It also means a thin comparable set when you come to value one, so both the upside and the pricing uncertainty are larger than on the Classic stock.

## What EDGE certification changes, and what it does not

Running costs, mostly, and that shows up in lettability rather than in price.

EDGE is the IFC's green building certification tool, which measures a building against benchmarks for energy use, water use and the embodied energy of its materials. Balwin builds to it across its portfolio, and the apartments here come Wi-Fi ready with eco-friendly appliances.

In a market shaped by load shedding and by water security, a building that uses less of both is easier to let and easier to justify at resale. What EDGE does not do is guarantee backup power or a borehole, which are separate specifications. Ask what the building actually has installed, in writing, rather than inferring it from the certificate. The [load shedding guide](/guides/load-shedding-property-cape-town/) and the [water security guide](/guides/cape-town-water-security-property/) cover what a Cape Town buyer should be checking.

## What to verify before you commit

Six checks, and the first two are the ones that move the numbers.

| Check | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Whether the letting rule is registered or contractual | Decides whether it binds future owners |
| Current levy and the reserve fund position | Sets your net yield, not your gross |
| What backup power and water the building actually has | EDGE does not imply either |
| Whether you are buying from Balwin or a resale seller | Decides whether transfer duty applies |
| Comparable long-term rents for the unit type | The only income route available here |
| The remaining build programme on the estate | Decides whether you live beside construction |
