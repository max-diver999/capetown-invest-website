The developer's own account of the project describes a process beginning over 6 years before delivery, in which the brothers looked internationally for a benchmark rather than at the Atlantic Seaboard around them, then consolidated what they found into one scheme. That is developer language, and the part of it a buyer can verify is the specification and the finish, which is what a viewing is for.

## The Modern or Classic choice, and what it does on resale

It turns interior style into a valuation variable.

Offering two interior treatments across the same layouts is generous at launch and consequential later. Two apartments of identical size on adjacent floors can present entirely differently, and buyer preference between the two is not evenly split in any market.

Three practical effects on a resale:

1. **Comparables inside the building are less comparable than the floor area suggests**, so a per-square-metre figure taken from a Modern unit may not describe a Classic one.
2. **Time on market can differ by style**, independently of price.
3. **Renovation cost differs**, because updating a Classic interior to current taste is a different job from refreshing a Modern one.

Insider tip: when a building offers two finish specifications, ask the managing agent which style has traded more often since completion. That single answer tells you which one the local market actually prefers, and no brochure will tell you.

## Buying into a six-year-old building

The body corporate record is the asset you are really assessing.

A 2020 scheme has now traded through several financial years, so its levies are actual rather than projected, its reserve fund is either funded or it is not, and its owners have had time to disagree about something. All of that is knowable before you offer.

Three documents answer it: the last two AGM minutes, the current levy with its history, and the reserve fund position. The [body corporate due diligence guide](/guides/body-corporate-due-diligence-cape-town/) covers how to read them and the [levies guide](/guides/sectional-title-levies-cape-town/) covers what the numbers should look like.

The cost stack also changes on a resale. Original sales from a VAT-registered developer carried the tax inside the price with no duty; a private purchase now attracts duty on the SARS scale at Atlantic Seaboard price levels, plus conveyancing. The [transfer cost calculator](/guides/transfer-duty-calculator/) prices that out at any figure.

## Why Fresnaye holds its level

Three demand sources at once, which is rare.

Fresnaye sits above the Sea Point strip: quieter than the promenade, close enough to use it, and residential in a way that much of the Seaboard is not. Demand arrives from local buyers, from semigration families moving down from Gauteng, and from foreign owners, and those three groups do not usually weaken at the same time.

That is the structural argument for the address, and it is a support rather than a guarantee. The [Fresnaye page](/areas/fresnaye-property-investment/) covers the local market and the [Atlantic Seaboard guide](/guides/atlantic-seaboard-property-investment-guide/) places it in the corridor.

## Advantages and disadvantages

The building's age produces both lists.

The advantages: a completed 2020 building with a real trading history rather than a projection, a Fresnaye address with three-way demand, more than 4,000 square metres of scheme rather than a single small block, and a specification the developer set deliberately high. The disadvantages: no developer stock, so you buy when an owner sells; a resale cost stack heavier at the front end than the original sales carried; and an interior-style split that makes in-building comparables less useful than they look.

For a foreign buyer the position is legally simple and financially constrained. There is no surcharge and no visa requirement, but lenders rarely fund past half the value for a non-resident, and funds should enter through an authorised dealer bank with the title endorsed non-resident, which the [foreigner guide](/guides/buy-cape-town-property-foreigner/) explains.

The developer's other Seaboard schemes are [Station House](/projects/station-house-fresnaye/), also in Fresnaye and completed in 2022, and [Mont Reve](/projects/mont-reve-sea-point/) in Sea Point, completed in 2025. The [Berman Brothers page](/developers/berman-brothers/) covers the record across all three.
