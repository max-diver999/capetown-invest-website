## What is left, and what it costs

Two of three buildings are gone before the third is released.

| Phase | Building | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One | Released | Sold out |
| Two | Released | Sold out |
| Three | East Bridge | Coming soon |

Rabie lists the development from R2m. On a scheme where two phases have already cleared, that figure describes the release rather than the remaining stock, so the practical question for a buyer now is which units East Bridge actually contains and at what price, not what the first phase opened at.

The alternative route is a resale from a phase one or two purchaser. That changes the tax position rather than the property: a developer sale buying new from the developer means the 15% VAT is already inside the figure quoted and duty does not arise, while a resale at R2m falls into the 6% transfer duty band with conveyancing restored. The [transfer cost calculator](/guides/transfer-duty-calculator/) works the difference out, and the [off-plan guide](/guides/off-plan-property-cape-town-guide/) covers cessions before transfer.

## The park frontage, and why it is the durable part

Because it is the one feature the next release cannot copy.

Rabie states that The Bridges is the final residential development with direct access to Ratanga Park's lawns, established trees and waterways. In a masterplanned precinct that keeps releasing phases, most advantages are temporary: finishes get matched, appliances get matched, even the security spec gets matched. Aspect over open space does not, because the open space is finite and this scheme takes the last of it.

From eight storeys the outlook extends past the park to the mountains and the Table Bay coastline. That is worth separating from the park frontage itself, because a view over a city can be built out and a view over a protected park generally cannot.

Insider tip: on any unit here, check what sits between your balcony and the park on the approved masterplan rather than on the site today. The question is not what you can see now, it is what the precinct is still entitled to build.

## Backup power is a letting feature, not a comfort one

In this market it decides whether a tenant can work from home.

Rabie specifies full backup power. In Cape Town that moves a building from the group a remote worker will not rent to the group they will, and it shows up in what tenants pay rather than in what they say they want. The [load shedding guide](/guides/load-shedding-property-cape-town/) covers what different backup specifications actually deliver.

The phrase deserves a question rather than a nod. Full backup can mean the common areas and lifts, or it can mean every socket in your apartment. Ask which circuits are covered and for how long the system is rated to run, in writing, because the two versions produce very different tenant experiences during a stage six week.

## Advantages and disadvantages

Three of each, and they are not symmetrical.

On the advantages: the park frontage cannot be repeated by a later release, the full backup power specification is a letting advantage in a load shedding market rather than a comfort item, and an entry point at R2m keeps the range accessible in a precinct that has been building since the early 2000s. On the disadvantages: two of the three phases are already sold, so choice is limited to East Bridge or a resale; eight storeys means lift dependency and a body corporate large enough to have politics; and Rabie keeps releasing in Century City, so in a year when several phases complete together, new supply lands on the same rental market at once.

## Where it sits against the rest of Century City

The accessible end of a precinct Rabie keeps building.

| Scheme | From | Status |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| The Bridges | R2m | Phase 3 to come |
| Park Place | R6.8m | 2 penthouses remaining |
| SkyWater | Not listed | Sold out in about eight months, under construction |

That spread is mostly unit mix. A studio in one building and a penthouse in another are not the same product, so read the table as a map of what each scheme leads with rather than as a quality ranking. The [Century City guide](/guides/century-city-property-investment-guide/) covers the precinct, and [Park Place](/projects/park-place-century-city/), [SkyWater](/projects/skywater-century-city/) and [On Park](/projects/on-park-century-city/) cover the neighbouring schemes.

The precinct's structural risk is the same thing that makes it work: Rabie keeps releasing, so new supply can pressure rents in a year when several phases complete together. Model on net rather than gross, allowing 8% to 10% of rent for a managing agent and one vacant month in 12 months. Rabie has not published a completion date for East Bridge, so treat any timeline you are given verbally as an estimate until it is in a contract.
