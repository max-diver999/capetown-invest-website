## What R754,900 actually buys

The lowest new-build entry in Balwin's Western Cape range, and a full amenity package with it.

| Unit | Bathrooms | From |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| One bedroom | One | R754,900 |
| Two bedroom | One or two | R1,269,900 |
| Three bedroom | Two or three | R1,529,900 |

The Green Collection is Balwin's entry tier, below the Classic Collection that carries [Suikerbos](/projects/suikerbos-balwin/) and [The Huntsman](/projects/the-huntsman-somerset-west/), and well below the Signature Collection. That naming describes where the price sits rather than what has been removed: the apartments are secure and ultra-modern, flow to a landscaped outdoor area, and the estate carries a Green Barn Lifestyle Centre with an indoor gym, a green cafe and a multi-purpose sports field.

Balwin covering the attorney and transfer cost lines matters proportionally more at this price than at any of its other schemes, because those costs are close to fixed while the purchase price is not. A private resale at R754,900 would sit below the R1,210,000 nil-rate threshold and attract no duty at all, but the conveyancing stack would return in full.

Insider tip: at the entry end of any Balwin release, ask how many one-bedroom units are in your building rather than in the estate. A block that is mostly one-beds behaves differently at re-let time from one where they are a minority.

## The location is the whole decision

Roughly 50 km from the CBD, at the far end of False Bay.

Gordon's Bay is a coastal Helderberg town past Somerset West with its own centre and its own economy. Treating it as an affordable Cape Town suburb is the mistake that this price invites, and it produces the wrong tenant and the wrong holding period.

Three consequences follow:

1. **The tenant pool is local**, weighted to people who live and work in the Helderberg, retirees and second-home owners rather than city commuters.
2. **The commute is a daily constraint**, not an occasional one, and a tenant who signs without testing it in traffic tends not to renew.
3. **The market is thinner**, so both letting and resale take longer than the same product would in the northern suburbs.

The [Somerset West page](/areas/somerset-west-property-investment/) covers the neighbouring Helderberg market and the [suburb price table](/guides/cape-town-property-prices-by-suburb-2026/) places the region against the metro.

## Short lets are closed, in a town that would want them

Which removes the model most buyers would reach for here.

A coastal town with a summer visitor season is exactly where nightly letting looks attractive, and Balwin does not permit it. The prohibition runs across the developer's schemes and the definition it uses appears in the developer's own words on our [Suikerbos page](/projects/suikerbos-balwin/), with the [body corporate Airbnb rules guide](/guides/body-corporate-airbnb-ban-rules/) covering how such rules are made binding.

So the income model is long-term rent from a local tenant, and the [long-term rental guide](/guides/long-term-rental-cape-town-guide/) sets out what that pays. Anyone underwriting this on holiday-let assumptions is underwriting a different building.

## Advantages and disadvantages at this price

The entry price creates both lists.

The advantages: the cheapest new-build entry Balwin offers in the province, a genuine amenity package rather than a stripped one, no attorney or transfer costs on the developer sale, and a 2026 purchase that puts a buyer into ownership at a figure most of the metro cannot match. The disadvantages: a location roughly 50 km from the city, a thin local rental market, no short-let option in a town built for visitors, and a Balwin estate that delivers many similar units into one small market at once.

## What decides the net

Four lines, and one is a forecast on a new scheme.

Agency management costs 8% to 10% of rent, reckon on a single empty month per 12 months, municipal rates run against the valuation roll, and the body corporate levy starts as a projection. The [levies guide](/guides/sectional-title-levies-cape-town/) covers what to ask for before signing.

For a foreign buyer, a first sale by the developer carries 15% VAT within the price and no duty falls due, while local banks seldom fund more than half the value for a non-resident, which at the one-bedroom entry means roughly R377,000 in cash before costs.
