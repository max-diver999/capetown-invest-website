Balwin advertises no attorney fees and no transfer costs on the developer sale. That is worth putting a number on rather than nodding at: on an ordinary R1,229,900 resale the buyer would face transfer duty plus conveyancing plus Deeds Office fees, and the [transfer cost calculator](/guides/transfer-duty-calculator/) works out what that stack comes to at any price. Removing it changes the cash required at transfer, not the price.

The developer also markets in phases rather than from a standing list, and a new phase was releasing at the time of writing. Phase pricing moves, so treat any figure on this page as the position on the date given at the bottom and confirm the live list before you budget.

## The rule that decides whether this works as an investment

No short-term letting, stated by the developer rather than discovered later.

Balwin defines short-term letting as the rental of a furnished residential property or room for a short duration, typically from a few nights up to six months, through platforms such as Airbnb and Booking.com, and does not permit it here. The [body corporate Airbnb rules guide](/guides/body-corporate-airbnb-ban-rules/) covers how such rules are normally imposed and what makes them binding.

For an investor the consequence is a different model entirely, not a smaller version of the same one:

1. **Income is monthly rent**, set by what a comparable one, two or three-bed lets for in the Sandown and Blouberg area, which the [long-term rental guide](/guides/long-term-rental-cape-town-guide/) sets out.
2. **Seasonality mostly disappears**, because a long lease does not care about January.
3. **Management is cheaper**, because a long-term managing agent costs a fraction of what short-let operations take.
4. **Vacancy risk concentrates**, because losing one long-term tenant costs a month or more rather than a weekend.

Insider tip: on a phased Balwin scheme, ask how many units in your building are investor-owned rather than owner-occupied. A block where most owners are letting long-term competes with itself on rent when several leases end in the same month.

## Where Sandown actually is, and who rents there

The northern coastal corridor, and the draw is price and beach rather than commute.

Sandown sits above Cape Town near the Blouberg and Table View area. The corridor's proposition is straightforward: newer stock, lower entry prices than the [Atlantic Seaboard](/guides/atlantic-seaboard-property-investment-guide/) or the [City Bowl](/guides/cape-town-city-bowl-property-investment/), and beach access without coastal pricing. The [Blouberg page](/areas/blouberg-property-investment/) covers the local market and the [suburb price table](/guides/cape-town-property-prices-by-suburb-2026/) places it against the rest of the metro.

The tenant pool follows from that. It is weighted to households buying space and safety per rand rather than proximity to the CBD, which produces steadier tenancies and lower rents than the central corridors. That is a coherent long-term letting market and a poor short-let one, which happens to align with what the scheme permits.

The trade-off is the commute. Anyone working in the CBD is looking at a drive on the R27 that behaves very differently at 07:30 than it does on a Sunday, and a tenant who discovers that after signing tends not to renew.

## What to check before signing on a new phase

Five things, and two of them are estimates dressed as figures.

| Check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| The levy, and the reserve fund plan behind it | On a phased scheme the levy starts as a projection |
| The completion date, in the contract rather than the brochure | Marketing dates are targets |
| NHBRC enrolment, confirmed in writing | It is the warranty on the build |
| Whether the letting rule is registered against the scheme | Registered rules bind future owners |
| Comparable long-term rents for your exact unit type | The only income route open here |

Two tax positions and one lending limit shape what this actually costs. Buying from Balwin, the price carries 15% VAT and no transfer duty, and the developer covers the attorney and transfer costs on top. A later resale of the same one-bedroom at R1,229,900 falls into the 3% transfer duty band with the conveyancing stack restored, which is the gap a buyer inherits on exit rather than on entry. A non-resident faces the further limit that South African banks typically lend near 50% loan-to-value. On the income side, build the model on net: a managing agent takes 8% to 10% of rent, and a vacancy allowance of one month in 12 months is the conservative starting assumption on new-build stock.

The levy point deserves the most attention. A new sectional title scheme launches its body corporate budget as a projection, and the first few years of actual costs decide whether that projection held. The [sectional title levies guide](/guides/sectional-title-levies-cape-town/) sets out what to ask for, and the [off-plan guide](/guides/off-plan-property-cape-town-guide/) covers the rest of the sequence from reservation to handover.
