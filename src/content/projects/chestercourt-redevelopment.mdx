## Golden Acre: 414 rentals above the commuter hub

Golden Acre's residential conversion puts 414 rental apartments directly above Cape Town's busiest taxi, rail and bus interchange, and the rent point is what makes it relevant to landlords nearby. Studios are reported from roughly R10,000 per month, around 30 percent below prevailing inner-city levels, with Neighbourgood running management and weekly servicing.

- 6,000 m² communal garden and clubhouse above the interchange
- Retail phases targeting an 18-hour precinct with better lighting and surveillance
- No units sold to private investors, so there is nothing here to acquire

For anyone holding a CBD studio or one-bed, the scheme is a new price anchor a tenant can quote back at renewal. Model 2027 rent against R10,000 plus servicing and amenity rather than against 2025 asking rents, because the comparison a tenant makes is total monthly cost against total monthly value, not rand per square metre. A serviced studio with weekly cleaning, a 6,000 m² garden and a clubhouse at R10,000 competes directly with an unserviced walk-up one-bed at the same rent, and the older building loses that comparison unless it discounts or upgrades. Owners of stock without parking, backup power or a lift should assume the gap shows up first at renewal rather than on new lettings, since sitting tenants shop before they sign again. Budget refurbishment capex or a 5 to 10 percent rent concession, and decide which before the first Golden Acre lease launches.

Retail phases pursue an 18-hour precinct with safer lighting, surveillance, and rationalised taxi flows per Engineering News reporting. Mayor Geordin Hill-Lewis has framed the project as CBD revitalisation — relevant context for owners nearby, even if you cannot buy a unit.

## 23 lower long: divercity affordable mixed-use

Divercity Urban Property Group, Ingenuity Property Investments, and Twin City are building a 12-storey mixed-use scheme at 23 Lower Long Street on land that housed a Virgin Active gym, valued near R69 million. The project delivers 416 affordable rental apartments with ground-floor retail, with construction underway and completion targeted end 2026.

Chief investment officer Jonathan Reader positions the scheme as inclusive city-centre housing for people who work downtown but commute from distant suburbs. Tri-Star Construction led demolition and foundations. The project follows Divercity's Herringbone Salt River collaboration — a pattern of institutional affordable rental rather than sectional title sell-off.

## Scenario workbook for cbd landlords

Scenario workbook for a CBD landlord is three rent cases run against the same unit, not a view on the market. Base case holds the current lease rate flat through 2027. Supply case cuts it 5 to 10 percent from the quarter Golden Acre and 23 Lower Long stabilise. Downside case adds a 2 month void plus letting commission.

Run each case through levies, municipal rates at roughly 0.69 cents per rand above the first R450,000 of value, and finance at prime near 10.5%, then read the net figure rather than the gross. The 830 new doors give a professional tenant a credible alternative at about R10,000 for a serviced studio, which is the mechanism behind the supply case.
