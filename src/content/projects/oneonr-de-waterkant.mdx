## How should buyers underwrite ONEONR in De Waterkant?

ONEONR matters because it puts one of Cape Town's most design-conscious apartment developers into one of the Atlantic Seaboard's most character-rich pockets for the first time. De Waterkant sits between the [City Bowl](/guides/cape-town-city-bowl-property-investment/) and [Green Point](/areas/green-point-property-investment/), a short walk from the V&A Waterfront and minutes from the restaurants and nightlife that define the [Atlantic Seaboard](/guides/atlantic-seaboard-property-investment-guide/) lifestyle. Blok's track record on [Sea Point](/areas/sea-point-property-investment/) buildings such as ONE46ONM and ONEHUNDREDONM shows what the studio does well: small-format, architecture-led sectional title in supply-constrained coastal streets where new land is scarce.

The investment case is location scarcity plus brand delivery, not a promised yield. Blok does not guarantee returns on ONEONR, and any rent or yield figure you see in your own modelling is MODELED and directional. Our working approach is to underwrite long-let on roughly 4% to 5.5% gross for comparable De Waterkant and Green Point stock, then compress to net after levies, rates, management, and vacancy. Treat anything above that as upside, never as the plan.

## What does the ONEONR development include?

ONEONR is Blok's 20th development and its first in De Waterkant: 74 units at 1 Rawbone Street across studios, one- and two-bedroom apartments, rowhouses and penthouses, priced from R1,995,000 to R16,695,000. Ground break is August 2025, completion targeted for Q3 2027, with a pool deck, 24-hour security and ground-floor café.

ONEONR is developed by Blok, the Cape Town studio behind a string of boutique apartment buildings on the Atlantic Seaboard and in the central city. This is Blok's 20th development and its first in De Waterkant, launched publicly in April 2025 with sales opening in May 2025. The building delivers 74 residential units across five product types: studios for the lowest entry point, one-bedroom apartments as the volume mid-range, two-bedroom apartments for owner-occupiers and family tenants, two-bedroom rowhouses with a townhouse feel, and two-bedroom penthouses at the top of the price ladder.

The design language deliberately preserves heritage elements in the façade while inserting Blok's contemporary apartment interiors. Marketing materials highlight time-aged heritage structures woven into the architecture, high-specification joinery, integrated oven and hob, air-conditioning in all apartments, and views oriented toward Table Mountain, Signal Hill, the harbour, and the Atlantic Ocean depending on aspect. Shared amenities include a communal pool deck, 24-hour on-site security, pet-friendly homes, a proposed community green space, and ground-floor café and shop space. Basement parking is available as an optional extra.

Construction is scheduled to break ground in August 2025, with completion estimated for the third quarter of 2027. That timeline places ONEONR firmly in the off-plan category, which means buyers carry construction and handover risk that near-complete buildings avoid. For the full framework on buying before completion, see our [off-plan property Cape Town guide](/guides/off-plan-property-cape-town-guide/) and the [new developments Cape Town 2026 roundup](/guides/new-developments-cape-town-2026/).

## Payment terms and purchase structure

ONEONR sells as new stock from a VAT-registered developer, so 15% VAT already sits inside the quoted price and no transfer duty is payable. On a R4,175,000 one-bedroom that removes a duty cheque of well over R200,000 that a resale buyer at the same price would have to fund in cash before registration.

What replaces it is a liquidity test. Cash buyers pay 30% of the purchase price on signature of the Offer to Purchase, so that same one-bedroom needs about R1,252,500 available at signing, with the balance drawn against the build programme through to Q3 2027. Non-residents financing locally face the usual ceiling near 50% loan-to-value and must show FICA-compliant source of funds on the offshore portion.

| Buyer type | Deposit on OTP | Finance | Tax treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cash buyer | 30% of purchase price | Balance per build schedule | 15% VAT in price, no transfer duty |
| Bond buyer | Per developer schedule | South African bank bond | 15% VAT in price, no transfer duty |
| Non-resident cash | 30% plus offshore proof | Often 50% local bond cap if financed | Same VAT rules, FICA on source of funds |

## What rental yield should ONEONR buyers model on net terms?

ONEONR is best underwritten as a long-let apartment in a walkable Atlantic Seaboard pocket, with optional short-stay upside if the body corporate and the City allow it. De Waterkant and adjacent Green Point one-bedroom stock typically MODELED around R18,000 to R28,000 per month in long-let rent depending on finish and aspect, while studios can sit lower and penthouses materially higher. These figures are market benchmarks, not ONEONR guarantees.

| Unit type | Indicative long-let rent (modelled) | Gross yield at mid price (modelled) | Net after costs (modelled) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Studio at R2,750,000 | R14,000 to R18,000/month | 6.1% to 7.9% gross | 3.5% to 5% net |
| One-bed at R4,175,000 | R22,000 to R28,000/month | 6.3% to 8.0% gross | 3.8% to 5.2% net |
| Two-bed at R7,475,000 | R32,000 to R42,000/month | 5.1% to 6.7% gross | 3% to 4.5% net |
| Penthouse at R14,445,000 | R55,000 to R75,000/month | 4.6% to 6.2% gross | 2.8% to 4% net |

Every figure in that table is modelled and directional. It is not guaranteed, not contractual, and not a forecast from Blok. Net yield compresses once you deduct the body corporate levy, municipal rates, insurance, vacancy, agent commission and maintenance, which is why a studio showing 6.1% to 7.9% gross lands at 3.5% to 5% net. Premium amenities such as the pool deck, 24-hour security and backup systems lift the levy above an older building without those services, and the penthouse tier carries the highest absolute levy against the thinnest tenant pool. Municipal rates are billed separately from the levy: the City exempts the first R450,000 of valuation and charges roughly 0.69 cents per rand a year above that. Request the draft body corporate budget and a three-year levy projection before you sign.

Insider tip: on an off-plan building that completes in one go, the real risk is the handover month rather than the rent. Seventy-four units transferring inside the same quarter puts a batch of near-identical studios onto the portals at once, and the first landlords to sign a tenant set the street rate everyone else has to match. Appoint a letting agent three months before the Q3 2027 completion window, not at occupation.

## What risks must ONEONR off-plan buyers verify before signing?

ONEONR off-plan buyers must confirm NHBRC enrolment, draft body corporate levy projections, short-stay rules in management conduct, staged payment schedules tied to August 2025 ground break through Q3 2027 completion, and FICA paths for non-residents before committing Blok's 30% cash deposit on the OTP.

Off-plan purchases fail when buyers skip verification, not when the developer lacks a brand. Before you sign at ONEONR, work through this checklist:

1. Confirm the approved building plans, NHBRC enrollment, and the construction programme from ground break (August 2025) to completion (Q3 2027).
2. Request the draft body corporate budget, levy schedule, and reserve fund plan including pool, security, and café common-area costs.
3. Verify whether short-term letting is permitted in the management rules, and read the City of Cape Town by-laws for your intended strategy.
4. Obtain a written payment schedule showing deposit, milestone calls, and handover balance for your buyer type.
5. For non-residents, confirm FICA documentation for offshore funds and the 50% typical local bond ceiling before you commit the 30% deposit.
6. Compare your modelled net yield against at least three live rental comparables in De Waterkant and Green Point for the same bedroom count.
7. Inspect the sale agreement for defect liability, snagging rights, and the NHBRC warranty period after registration.

## Who ONEONR suits

Four buyer profiles are a fit at ONEONR: investors taking studios from R1,995,000 for long-let, CBD professionals buying one- and two-bedroom stock for owner-use, family landlords in rowhouses at R7,625,000, and view-led capital buyers in penthouses to R16,695,000 who accept net yield near 2.8% to 4%.

**The Atlantic Seaboard investor** wants Blok design in a walkable coastal pocket without paying Sea Point peak pricing. Studios and one-bedroom units fit this profile if net yield works at conservative modelled rents.

**The CBD professional owner-occupier** values a ten-minute walk to the Foreshore and City Bowl offices, with Green Point nightlife on the doorstep. One-bedroom and two-bedroom apartments suit owner-use with occasional letting.

**The family or dual-income tenant landlord** targets two-bedroom apartments or rowhouses for longer lease terms and lower turnover. Underwrite on long-let only unless short-stay rules are confirmed in writing.

**The view-led capital buyer** focuses on upper-floor and penthouse stock for Table Mountain and harbour aspects. Accept lower modelled yield per rand in exchange for scarcity and owner-use appeal.

**The foreign cash buyer** can purchase with no buyer surcharge, but must plan the 30% deposit, FICA proof of offshore funds, and typically a 50% bond cap if financing locally. Read the [foreign buyer hub](/guides/buy-cape-town-property-foreigner/), the [cost of buying guide](/guides/cost-of-buying-property-cape-town/), and the [rental yield guide](/guides/cape-town-rental-yield-guide/) before you offer.

ONEONR is not the right building if you need a guaranteed yield, a handover inside 12 months, or the lowest possible levy. It is a coherent Blok bet on De Waterkant scarcity if your numbers work on modelled net rent and you verify the off-plan terms before you sign.
